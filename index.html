A stylized 3D lifestyle prototype. Your apartment breathes with the day. Everything here is simulated fiction — no real location, no GPS, no network.

  • Look: drag the screen to rotate the camera
  • Interact: near an object, press E or tap INTERACT
  • Time: drag the clock to move through day & night
  • Avatar: open Dress Up to change body, hair and clothes
  • Leave home: walk to the front door and choose Go Outside
